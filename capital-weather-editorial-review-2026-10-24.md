## Decisions and revised counts

The standard is evidence of the activity taking place outdoors. An outdoor-sounding title, an outdoor-capable venue, outdoor seating, or parking availability alone does not qualify an event. A multi-part indoor program cannot qualify merely because it mentions an outdoor sub-event.

## Corrections in the widget

- Removed Countdown's all-day occurrence with an explicit editorial exclusion. Added a general guard against qualifying mixed site-wide house-tour/exhibit programs through an outdoor side activity.
- Replaced the new adapters' title/venue-only classification fallback with activity-description evidence. Park names, farmers-market titles, incidental outdoor seating and free parking are no longer sufficient on their own.
- Recorded the reviewed source, title, venue, evidence and primary link for each investigated series. Reviews are scoped to those identities. Rich-feed entries additionally must retain the relevant outdoor activity wording in their current descriptions; for example, King & Rye no longer qualifies if courtyard evidence disappears. CTY's compact rows have no descriptions, so their linked activity descriptions were reviewed directly.
- Added concise outdoor/indoor-contingency text to the existing card location line for King & Rye and Lost Boy. The full location remains in the tooltip and detail panel; full contingency text appears in details. Existing fixed card dimensions are preserved.
- Added regression coverage for a park venue without activity evidence, an outdoor-sounding title, incidental seating, parking, mixed site-wide programs and lost courtyard evidence. Captured fixtures still include Countdown, so the browser test proves that the parser excludes it.
- Preserved five source adapters, six healthy direct requests, the three-worker pool, eight-second deadlines, pagination caps, progressive rendering, deduplication, daily tabs, event details and the forecast selection contract. There are no added per-event network requests.
