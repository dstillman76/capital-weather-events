## Editorial safeguards and data limitations

- New classification uses activity descriptions and actual route/terrain statements. It never qualifies an event from its title, park name, outdoor seating, parking or category alone. No hardcoded list of approved round-2 events exists in production code.
- NPS `expandRecurring=true` returns explicit dated occurrences. The parser uses each record's `date`, checks `cancelledDateList`, and iterates its actual time slots. It does not expand recurrence rules or copy every `dates[]` entry onto every record. Seven canceled dates are removed in this snapshot. Sunrise/sunset placeholders, missing schedules and incompatible stated minute-duration versus API times fail closed.
- NPS indoor mill/house tours, the nature talk whose only outdoor evidence is a porch meeting point, and ambiguous memorial programs are excluded. The National Mall's **Unfinished America** walking tour is also excluded: 9:30–10:30 a.m. in the API conflicts with its 90-minute description. No replacement end time was guessed.
- FONA event cards have epoch timestamps plus malformed timezone labels such as `-4:00`. Epochs are converted to America/New_York, with both start and end required to agree with the published local date/clock. Subtitles and a matching live feature-description block establish the activities. Unrelated page copy is not used as evidence. Future cards outside the window are filtered before inclusion.
- PG's empty venue arrays are not silently assigned the agency headquarters. A venue must come from the response's real venue object, an explicit named park after `@` in the published title, or a location statement in the description. **The title is used to read a location only; separate description evidence must establish that the activity is outdoors.** The five selected publisher pages independently confirmed those locations. Unknown city/coordinates remain empty rather than being invented. Watkins' farm-market body supplies its specific 601 Watkins Park Drive address; the park's generic contact address is not substituted.
- Sixty-two PG records lack a resolvable location under those rules; many are indoor records anyway. They are not counted as 62 missed outdoor events. QR/trail sessions and a campfire program without a location in the browser-readable data were not added merely because their activities sounded outdoors.
- The existing King & Rye courtyard contingency, Lost Boy mixed-venue note and Countdown exclusion remain unchanged. New weather notes preserve published cancellation/indoor-relocation conditions. The Civil War walk's bad-weather cancellation condition is visible through the existing note/detail UI.
- Same-title, same-date sessions with independently supplied start times and venues retain separate IDs. For example, Watkins' 5–6 p.m. and 6–7 p.m. classes are distinct verified sessions with distinct publisher schedules. Duplicate time/venue records collapse. Established sources retain their prior title/date/geography behavior and win any overlap with round-2 records regardless of arrival order. No overlap was found among the 19 additions and the 27 live baseline records in this window.

## Performance and request budgets

The round-2 sources share the existing **three-worker direct-source pool**, rather than adding another competing pool. Each request has the existing eight-second timeout and 1.8 MB response guard. NPS and Tribe discovery have a three-page cap and preserve completed records if a later page fails. FONA is one bounded page request. Each completed source renders independently. Bethesda's old proxy discovery is replaced by its direct API, with its source name retained; its old parser remains in the file for historical compatibility but is not called in parallel as a duplicate discovery path.

Healthy round-2 request count in this window: **6** (NPS 1+1, PG 2, FONA 1, Bethesda 1). No new proxy, API key, paid service, backend or deployment infrastructure is introduced. Existing unrelated source queues and their caps remain intact.
