Join the National Geographic Society, the Embassy of Finland and the Italian Cultural Institute for a special screening of the acclaimed documentary "Aalto: Architect of Emotions" (2020).

Dec 04, 2026. From: 6:30 PM to 9:00 PM

Join the National Geographic Society, the Embassy of Finland and the Italian Cultural Institute for a special screening of the acclaimed documentary "Aalto: Architect of Emotions" (2020) at the Museum of Exploration in Washington, D.C.

During this film, you'll explore the captivating life, enduring legacy and visionary design philosophy of architect Alvar Aalto — one of Europe's greatest modern architects — and his collaborators, architects Aino Aalto and Elissa Aalto.

Before the screening, hear from a panel of leading architecture experts as they explore the couple's landmark works, design philosophy and profound Italian design influences.
